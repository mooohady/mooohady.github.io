Support

Buzzie is operated by Mohamed Ahmed Abdelhady in his personal capacity, in Egypt. Contact buzziehelp@gmail.com for account access, technical help, privacy requests, safety reports, or appeals.

You can pause or delete your account, block users, and report content in the app. If you cannot access your account, email us. Include the account email and a short description of the issue. Do not send a password, identity document, private conversation, or medical record unless we explain why specific information is necessary through a verified support exchange.

For a privacy request, tell us whether you want access, correction, deletion, to object to processing, or to withdraw consent where consent applies. We may ask for proportionate identity verification to protect your account. Egyptian privacy requests are subject to a six-working-day response deadline. An appeal should identify the relevant decision and explain why you want it reviewed.

Automated checks can help flag incoming requests and approaching deadlines. They do not provide continuous monitoring or replace the operator's responsibility to verify requests and decide disputed safety or privacy cases. Buzzie does not promise round-the-clock support.

For immediate danger, users should contact local emergency services or appropriate animal-welfare authorities. Buzzie is not an emergency service.
