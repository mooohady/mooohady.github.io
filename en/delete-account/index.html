Delete your Buzzie account

You can request permanent deletion without reinstalling Buzzie or signing in to the app.

Request deletion by email

Email buzziehelp@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Buzzie account". Use the email connected to your Buzzie account and include your Buzzie username if you know it. Tell us that you want your account and associated data deleted. If you cannot use that email, explain this so we can arrange proportionate verification.

We will verify account ownership before processing the request. Do not send passwords or identity documents unsolicited. Deleting an account is permanent; pausing an account or uninstalling Buzzie does not delete it.

Delete from the app

Open Profile → Settings → Account & sign-in → Delete account, then follow the confirmation steps.

What happens to your data

Deletion starts removal of the account, associated pet/profile information and uploaded media. Depending on the record, related Community, adoption and chat data is deleted or its identifying link is removed. Your messages are deleted and your chats disappear for the other person too. Copies another person saved or shared are outside Buzzie's control.

Limited security, support or enforcement records may remain where a specific case, abuse prevention or a legal obligation requires them; safety reports are kept without the link to your deleted account. We will explain any such reason when we answer your request. Backup copies can still contain your data after deletion; if one is ever restored, your deletion is applied again. Some providers keep their own technical records for a limited time under their own policies.

For the full explanation of retention, rights and provider processing, read the Privacy Notice. For help with a deletion request, contact buzziehelp@gmail.com.
