Community Guidelines

Status: pre-launch enforcement policy, version 0.1.

Protect people and animals

Do not threaten, harass, stalk, shame, exploit, sexualize, or expose private information. Never promote cruelty, neglect, animal fighting, poisoning, abandonment, coercive mating, medically dangerous practices, or trade that violates law.

Be authentic and useful

Do not impersonate people, misrepresent ownership or health, run scams, manipulate engagement, send repetitive solicitations, or use deceptive links. Only upload content you are authorized to share.

Keep sensitive information private

Do not publish exact home locations, private contact details, identity documents, payment credentials, medical records, or another person's personal data. Move carefully before sharing contact information in private chat.

How enforcement works

People can report content or profiles and block users immediately. Moderators classify severity, preserve the minimum evidence required, record a reason code, and choose no action, content removal, warning, feature restriction, suspension, or permanent ban. Imminent threats to a person or animal are escalated immediately to the designated safety lead and, when legally appropriate, relevant authorities.

Reports are not automatic guilt findings. Decisions should use available context, prior history, proportionality, repeat behavior, and risk. Except where unsafe or legally restricted, affected users receive a concise notice and an appeal route.

Response targets

  • Imminent danger, credible threats, animal cruelty, sexual exploitation, or exposed highly sensitive data: acknowledge and begin review within 30 minutes during coverage.
  • Scams, impersonation, targeted harassment, or repeated evasion: within 4 hours during coverage.
  • Spam and lower-risk content disputes: within 24 hours.

These are operational targets, not guarantees. Public store launch must not begin without named coverage for every published coverage window.
