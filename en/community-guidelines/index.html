Community Guidelines

Status: pre-launch enforcement policy, version 0.1.

Protect people and animals

Do not threaten, harass, stalk, shame, exploit, sexualize, or expose private information. Never promote cruelty, neglect, animal fighting, poisoning, abandonment, coercive mating, medically dangerous practices, or trade that violates law.

Be authentic and useful

Do not impersonate people, misrepresent ownership or health, run scams, manipulate engagement, send repetitive solicitations, or use deceptive links. Only upload content you are authorized to share.

Child safety

Buzzie prohibits child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE), including child sexual abuse material (CSAM), grooming and solicitation. Accounts are restricted to adults aged 18 or over.

Report concerns through the in-app report controls or contact Mohamed Ahmed Abdelhady at buzziehelp@gmail.com. Do not forward suspected illegal material. On obtaining actual knowledge of CSAM, Buzzie will restrict access, remove the material and report confirmed cases to the appropriate regional authority as required by law. Necessary evidence is handled with restricted access. Buzzie is not an emergency service.

Keep sensitive information private

Do not publish exact home locations, private contact details, identity documents, payment credentials, medical records, or another person's personal data. Move carefully before sharing contact information in private chat.

How enforcement works

People can report content or accounts and block users immediately. We review the available context, keep necessary evidence with restricted access, and may remove content, warn users, restrict features, suspend or close accounts, or take no action. Urgent safety concerns take priority and may be referred to the appropriate authorities where lawful.

Reports are not automatic guilt findings. Decisions should use available context, prior history, proportionality, repeat behavior, and risk. Except where unsafe or legally restricted, affected users receive a concise notice and an appeal route.

We do not promise continuous monitoring or a fixed moderation response time. For immediate danger, contact local emergency services or the appropriate animal-welfare authority.
