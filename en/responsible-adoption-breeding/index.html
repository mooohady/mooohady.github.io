Responsible Adoption and Breeding

Last updated: 10 October 2026.

Adoption

  • Put welfare and long-term fit ahead of speed, money, appearance, or pressure.
  • Verify the person and their authority to rehome the animal. Meet in a safe place and never send money solely on the basis of a profile.
  • Share material health, vaccination, sterilization, behavior, bite, disability, and care information honestly, with veterinary records only through an appropriate private channel.
  • Use a written handover record, discuss return arrangements, and plan transport and a gradual transition.
  • Never offer or seek animals for fighting, cruelty, abandonment, illegal trade, coercion, or experimentation.

Breeding

  • Buzzie discovery is not veterinary or breeding approval. Consult a licensed veterinarian and obtain breed-appropriate genetic, infectious-disease, and reproductive screening.
  • Do not breed animals that are underage, medically unfit, closely related where harmful, coerced, or likely to pass severe avoidable suffering.
  • Avoid repeated or unsafe pregnancies. Plan emergency veterinary care, suitable homes, identification, vaccination, and lifelong responsibility for every offspring.
  • Do not guarantee pregnancy, litter size, traits, purity, or health. Disclose known risks and do not use exploitative deposits or pressure.
  • Animal fighting, sexualized animal content, coercive mating, and breeding for harmful traits or illegal purposes result in removal and potential escalation.

Enforcement

Users may report welfare concerns with the "Animal welfare" report reason. Moderators prioritize imminent risk, preserve limited evidence, and apply the Community Guidelines. Buzzie may remove listings or profiles and restrict or suspend accounts. Users remain responsible for all veterinary, licensing, consumer, contract, and animal-welfare obligations.
