Privacy Notice

Effective date: 7 October 2026.

Who is responsible

Mohamed Ahmed Abdelhady operates Buzzie in his personal capacity in Egypt and controls its personal-data processing. Contact buzziehelp@gmail.com for privacy, support, safety reports and appeals. This notice covers Buzzie accounts, the mobile app and its support service.

What we collect and why

  • Account and sign-in: identifiers, name, email, chosen username, provider identity, authentication/session records and eligibility confirmation. These support access, account recovery and the service you request. Apple or Google handles its own sign-in under its own terms; Buzzie does not receive your Apple or Google password.
  • Adult eligibility: you enter your own date of birth to check that you are at least 18. It is not stored in the account profile; the eligibility confirmation time and policy version are retained. This is a declaration, not identity-document verification.
  • Your activity: owner and pet profiles, photos, discovery preferences, adoption listings and requests, community posts/comments/stories, reactions, connections, message requests, private messages/media, blocks and reports. We process these to provide the features you choose and enforce their safety rules.
  • Location: after you choose to use location and grant foreground permission, your device supplies its current location. Before transmission for storage, the app rounds latitude and longitude to two decimal places; the server also applies rounding. We retain that point and a broad area label for nearby discovery and adoption. Rounding reduces accuracy but does not make a location anonymous. Other users see an area and approximate distance, not your coordinates. There is no background location tracking or manual location-entry option. Permission can be withdrawn in device settings; nearby features then require location again.
  • Notifications: if you opt in, we process notification preferences, a device/installation identifier, push token and delivery records to send service notifications. Turn them off in the app or device settings.
  • Security and support: technical request logs, limited crash/error information, support correspondence and the evidence needed to investigate abuse or exercise privacy rights. Network providers may receive an IP address to deliver requests. Diagnostic filtering excludes private chat text, precise coordinates, health notes, private media links and report content from Buzzie's error-event payloads.

Necessary account, content and communication processing supports the service requested under the Terms. Location and optional notifications use your permission and choice. Proportionate abuse prevention and security protect the operator's lawful rights while respecting users' fundamental rights. Processing specifically required by law, a competent authority or a court uses that requirement. Where consent is required, it must be obtained for the stated purpose; accepting Terms or acknowledging this notice does not authorize unrelated processing or replace a required permit.

Product analytics is disabled for the initial release. Buzzie does not use advertising identifiers, session replay or sell personal data. Enabling additional optional processing would require an updated notice and any necessary consent mechanism first.

Community safety checks: before publishing a Community post, story, comment or caption edit, we submit its public text and selected photos to OpenAI's moderation API. This check does not submit private chats, saved discovery coordinates or account contact fields. Uploaded content can itself contain personal information, so share only material you are entitled to publish. We keep a content fingerprint or immutable photo reference and an allow/block result to enforce the check. Automated checks can miss harmful content or reject acceptable content; reporting and appeals remain available through the app and support. Community video publishing is unavailable in this beta.

Who can receive data

Eligible app users see the profile, pet and community/listing content you choose to share. Message participants receive the messages and media you send them. Private conversations are not end-to-end encrypted: authorized service operations and infrastructure providers can process them for delivery, security, rights requests and lawful obligations. Do not upload identity documents, payment credentials, human medical records or another person's private information without lawful authority.

Buzzie uses Supabase for account, database and private media storage in the selected EU region, Ireland; Expo for app builds and push routing; Google/Firebase Cloud Messaging for Android push; Apple for Apple sign-in and iOS push; Sentry's EU service for filtered error diagnostics; Google Gmail for support; and OpenAI for Community text/photo safety classification. GitHub Pages serves the public information/policy website. These providers may use international subprocessors and infrastructure, including outside Egypt and outside the EU. The selected database region is not a promise that every provider processes data only in Ireland.

Providers receive the data required for their role and operate under applicable service/data-processing terms. Apple and Google also act under their own policies for their accounts and store services. Disclosure to authorities requires an applicable legal obligation or another lawful ground. International processing and transfers are subject to applicable Egyptian requirements. This notice describes the service and does not state that a government permit has been granted.

Retention and deletion

Active account and feature data is retained while needed to provide the chosen service. Pausing an account restricts its use and visibility; it does not erase it. Deletion in Settings starts the server account/media deletion process. Depending on the record, related data is deleted or its identifying link is removed. Copies already saved or shared by another person are outside Buzzie's deletion control.

A story's expiry ends its normal availability; it does not establish erasure of every stored copy. Security, support and enforcement records may be retained only while an identified case, necessary abuse-prevention purpose or applicable legal obligation requires them. A hold must identify its reason, scope and review/end condition; unnecessary personal data must be removed or irreversibly anonymized.

Backups are restricted recovery copies and are not used for normal account access. They can retain earlier data while needed for recovery or an identified legal obligation. Recovery copies must be reviewed and removed when that purpose ends. A restoration must reapply subsequent account-deletion decisions before returning to service. We do not promise immediate erasure from every recovery copy or an expiry interval that has not been established.

Removing a Buzzie account or push token does not itself prove erasure of all provider logs or Firebase installation records. The operator must handle applicable provider deletion requests separately and confirm completion. Support requests are retained only as needed to resolve the request and any identified legal or dispute obligation. We do not describe a provider's query window as an erasure deadline.

Your rights, choices and complaints

You may request information about your data, access/a copy, correction, deletion, restriction to a specified scope, withdrawal of consent where used, and objection to processing or its results when they conflict with your fundamental rights. Use app settings for available account/profile controls or email buzziehelp@gmail.com; requests do not require access to a working app account. We may ask for proportionate verification, preferably through the existing account channel. Do not send a password or identity document unsolicited.

We must respond within the applicable Egyptian deadline, including the six-working-day determination period for an applicable Article 10 data-access/disclosure request. If a request cannot be fulfilled, we will explain the applicable reason and complaint route. Withdrawal does not invalidate earlier lawful processing; a legal obligation may require limited continued retention. If a data breach concerns your personal data, we will notify you and the competent authority as required by law. You may complain to Egypt's Personal Data Protection Center at https://pdpc.gov.eg/.

Buzzie is for adults aged 18 or over and offers no guardian-consent route. Suspected underage accounts may be restricted and their data handled through the applicable deletion/safety process. We will give notice of material policy changes and obtain fresh consent where required. The effective date of this version appears above.
