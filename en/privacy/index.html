Privacy Notice

Status: pre-launch draft for Egyptian legal review. Effective date must be set only when published. This is product documentation, not a legal opinion.

Who controls the data

Buzzie is operated and its data processing is controlled by Mohamed Ahmed Abdelhady in his personal capacity, residing in Egypt. Privacy and support requests may be sent to buzziehelp@gmail.com. Applicable Egyptian data-processing permits and international-transfer authorization remain unresolved. This notice must not be represented as final or legally approved.

Data Buzzie processes

Buzzie may process account identifiers and authentication records; an account holder's date of birth transiently to check 18+ eligibility; the resulting confirmation time and policy version; owner and pet profiles; pet photos; approximate discovery location and preferences; community posts, comments, stories, reactions, blocks and reports; private messages and requested media; adoption listings and requests; account lifecycle records; and security, diagnostic, and privacy-limited product events. The submitted account-holder date of birth is validated and then discarded; it is not retained in the account record.

Exact coordinates are used to calculate discovery results and are not intended for public display. Private chats, exact coordinates, health notes, private media URLs, and report details are excluded from product analytics.

Why it is used

Data is used to authenticate users, provide discovery and communication, support adoption and responsible pet connections, prevent abuse, investigate reports, secure the service, operate account deletion, meet legal duties, and understand activation and retention using limited event data.

The final legal review must map every purpose to the lawful basis required by Egypt's Personal Data Protection Law and applicable executive regulations, including any consent, sensitive-data, child-data, cross-border-transfer, processor, and direct-marketing obligations.

Sharing and international processing

Approved infrastructure, authentication, email, error-monitoring, and analytics processors may receive only the data needed for their role. The production processor list, hosting regions, transfer mechanism, retention terms, and contracts must be completed before enabling providers. Buzzie does not sell personal data.

Retention

Operational data is retained only for documented product, safety, security, legal, and dispute needs. Account deletion removes or anonymizes user data through the tested deletion workflow, subject to narrowly documented legal or safety holds. Backup expiry and restoration procedures are described in the internal recovery runbook.

User choices and rights

Users can edit profiles, block and report users, pause their account, and request account deletion in the app. Send access, correction, deletion, objection, consent-withdrawal, complaint, or appeal requests to buzziehelp@gmail.com. We may require proportionate identity verification. Egyptian privacy requests are subject to a six-working-day response deadline. Provider-event erasure currently requires a separate operator step; app deletion alone does not yet remove PostHog events.

Age eligibility

Buzzie accounts are for people aged 18 or older. The onboarding check is a self-declaration and is not identity or age-document verification. Buzzie does not offer a guardian-consent route for minors. If Buzzie reasonably believes an account holder is under 18, the account may be restricted or removed and the related data handled under the deletion, safety, and legal-retention rules in this notice.

Contact and complaints

Operator: Mohamed Ahmed Abdelhady. Privacy and support requests: buzziehelp@gmail.com. You may also complain to Egypt's Personal Data Protection Center; its public website is https://pdpc.gov.eg/. The final notice must identify the applicable transfer authorization and complete the processing-basis and retention details before public registration opens.
